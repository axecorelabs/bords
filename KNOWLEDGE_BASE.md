## 1. What is Bords?

Bords is a **work operating system** — a structured platform where teams design how they operate, embed that design into organized systems, and interact with it through an AI layer that makes organizational knowledge accessible, actionable, and scalable.

Most teams suffer from the same problem: communication is scattered across chat apps, plans live in documents no one reads, processes exist only in people's heads, and institutional knowledge disappears when people leave. Bords is built to solve that at the root.

Work in Bords is organized into **boards** that act as living systems — they house conversations, plans, processes, and institutional knowledge in a way that remains accessible and useful long after it was created. Boards aren't just canvases. They're the structural unit of how your organization thinks and operates.

At the center of the platform is **Bords AI** — a context-aware assistant that doesn't just answer questions or help you write. It actively participates in building your workspace. Bords AI can create boards for you, collaborate on structured plans, and translate those plans into detailed standard operating procedures (SOPs) that get organized directly into boards. Ideas become structured, executable systems without any manual formatting work.

Over time, as your team builds and refines more workflows, SOPs, and institutional knowledge inside Bords, the platform evolves into your organization's **company brain** — a central repository of how your company thinks, decides, and operates. Bords AI can interpret this context to answer questions about processes, explain decisions, and help any team member understand not just *what* to do, but *why* it is done that way and how their role connects to the larger structure.

This also creates a powerful model for knowledge transfer. Consultants and operators can design complete operational systems — encoded as boards with structured SOPs — and deploy them into organizations. Bords AI then guides teams through these systems, turning static documentation into an interactive learning and execution environment.

**Core positioning:** Bords sits at the intersection of collaboration software and intelligent operational infrastructure. It is not a task manager. It is not a note-taking app. It is the platform where organizations design how they work — and where that design becomes something teams can actually use.

## 3. Getting Started

### Creating an account
Sign up at [bords.app](https://bords.app) with your email address or continue with Google. After signing up with email you'll receive a verification link — click it to activate your account.

Use your email and password, or click **Continue with Google** for one-click sign-in.

After signing in you land on your personal canvas workspace. Click **New Board** to create your first board, give it a name, and start adding blocks.

## 4. Workspaces

Bords has two workspace types. You can switch between them at any time using the **Workspace Switcher** in the top bar.

### Personal Workspace
Your private productivity space. Boards here are visible only to you unless you explicitly share them. Use it for personal projects, private planning, or anything you're not ready to share with a team.

### Organization Workspace
A shared space for teams. Create an organization, invite members, and everyone works under the same context.

- **Roles**: Owner · Admin · Member — each with appropriate permissions
- **Team boards**: Boards created within an org are accessible to org members
- **Board access control**: Fine-grained sharing within the org
- **Centralized management**: Add or remove members, change roles, manage boards from one dashboard

You can belong to multiple organizations and switch between them instantly.

## 5. The Board Canvas

Every board is an **infinite canvas** — there are no page boundaries. You can zoom in, zoom out, and pan in any direction. Items sit at fixed positions on the canvas; you drag them wherever you want.

### Navigating the canvas
| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Pan (move around) | Hold **Space** + drag, or use the Hand tool |
| Zoom in/out | Scroll wheel, or pinch on trackpad/touch |
| Select an item | Click it |
| Select multiple items | Click + drag a selection box |
| Move an item | Drag it |
| Resize an item | Drag its resize handle |

### Grid & snap
Toggle the **Grid** from the dock to show a background grid. When the grid is on, items snap to it as you move them — useful for keeping layouts tidy.

### Canvas backgrounds
Choose a custom background for any board from the background picker: solid colors, patterns, gradients, or your own uploaded image.

## 6. Canvas Blocks

Drop any of these modular blocks onto your board. Every block can be placed anywhere, resized, and moved at any time.

### Sticky Notes
Quick-capture notes with customizable colors. Click a sticky note to open the full editing modal where you can write longer content, add formatting, or link to other items.

### Checklists
To-do lists with completable items. Each item supports:
- A due date and time
- Email reminders
- Assignment to a team member (org boards)
- Automatic sync when a teammate completes their copy

### Kanban Boards
Full kanban columns with draggable task cards. Each card supports:
- Title and description
- Assignee
- Due date
- Priority level (low / normal / high)
- Completion status

Build entire project workflows directly on the canvas without leaving the board.

### Text Blocks
Freeform rich text placed anywhere. Write long-form notes, documentation, meeting agendas, or instructions right alongside your visual elements.

### Media
Upload images and files directly to the canvas. Drag and drop a file onto the board or use the media button. Files are stored securely in the cloud and rendered inline.

Supported: images (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, SVG), documents, and more.

### Tables
Structured tabular data right on the canvas. Useful for data that doesn't fit neatly into sticky notes or kanban cards.

### Drawings
Freehand drawing and annotation powered by tldraw. Sketch ideas, annotate screenshots, draw arrows between elements, highlight important areas, or just doodle.

### Connections (Connection Lines)
Draw lines between any two items on the canvas to show relationships, flows, or dependencies. Customize line style, color, and arrow heads from the connection modal.

### Reminders
Attach a time-based reminder to any checklist item. When the deadline approaches, Bords sends you an email notification so nothing gets missed.

## 8. Tasks & Assignments

### How task assignment works
Tasks on Bords are bidirectionally synced — the board is the source of truth.

1. **Create** a checklist item or kanban card on your board
2. **Assign** it to a team member
3. **Publish** — push the assignment to the cloud so the member receives it on their board
4. **Track** — watch status update as the member moves the task through Draft → Assigned → Completed
5. **Sync** — when your teammate completes the task, it marks as complete on your board automatically too

No manual status chasing. No copy-pasting updates. The boards stay in sync.

### Task priorities
Every task has a priority: **Low**, **Normal**, or **High**. High-priority tasks are flagged in red across the board, dashboard, and notification views.

### Due dates & overdue alerts
Assign a due date to any task or checklist item. Overdue items surface prominently in dashboards and are highlighted in red so you always know what needs immediate attention.

## 9. Real-Time Collaboration

Bords is built multiplayer from the ground up.

### Live cursors
See your collaborators' cursors moving on the board in real time. Names appear next to each cursor so you always know who's doing what.

### Simultaneous editing
Multiple people can edit the same board at the same time. Add a sticky note, move a card, check off a task — all changes appear for everyone instantly.

Under the hood, Bords uses **CRDTs (Conflict-free Replicated Data Types)** to ensure simultaneous edits never corrupt each other.

### Offline support
Lose your internet connection? No problem. Bords stores your board locally in your browser. Keep working, and when you reconnect all your changes merge seamlessly with the server — no data loss, no conflicts.

### Comments
Leave threaded comments on any board. Discuss changes, ask questions, and keep conversations in context, right where the work is happening. No need to switch to a chat app for board-specific discussions.

### Active collaborators
An avatar row in the top bar shows everyone currently viewing or editing the board. Hover to see their name.

## 16. Account & Security

### Authentication methods
- **Email + password** — with email verification on sign-up
- **Google OAuth** — one-click sign-in via your Google account

### Password management
Change your password any time in Settings. If you've forgotten it, use the **Forgot password** link on the login page.

### Session security
Sessions are managed with secure, httpOnly cookies. You can sign out at any time from the top bar.

### Data privacy
- Your personal boards are private by default — nobody can access them without explicit sharing
- Organization data is scoped to members of that organization only
- All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored encrypted at rest

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use Bords for free?**
Yes. Bords offers a free tier. Paid plans unlock additional features — see the [Pricing page](https://bords.app/pricing) for current plan details.

**How many boards can I create?**
Limits depend on your plan. Check the Pricing page for the latest board limits per tier.

**Can I use Bords offline?**
Yes. Bords works offline using local browser storage. Your changes sync back to the cloud automatically when your connection is restored.

**How many people can collaborate on a board at once?**
Multiple people can edit a board simultaneously. There is no hard cap on concurrent collaborators.

**What happens if two people edit the same item at the same time?**
Bords uses CRDTs to automatically resolve simultaneous edits without conflicts. You won't overwrite each other's work.

**Can I invite someone who doesn't have a Bords account?**
Yes. Send them an invitation link. They'll be prompted to create a free account before accessing the shared board.

**Is my data backed up?**
Yes. Board data is persisted to the cloud whenever you publish or sync. Your boards are not at risk if you close the browser or lose connection.

**Can I belong to more than one organization?**
Yes. You can be a member of multiple organizations and switch between them using the Workspace Switcher.

**How do I delete a board?**
Open the board, go to the top bar menu, and select **Delete board**. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

**How do I contact support?**
Reach us at [support@bords.app](mailto:support@bords.app) or use the in-app feedback option.
